What is a token?

  • 🪙

    A token is a digital receipt. It shows who owns what.

  • 🧱

    Tokenizing = putting a real thing on it. Gold, a bond, a fund share.

  • ⚠︎

    Different risks. The issuer can fail. Keys can be lost.

  • 🔎

    Always ask: what do I really own?

“Risk comes from not knowing what you are doing.”Warren Buffett

Not advice.

Wallets

  • 🔑

    A wallet keeps your secret key. The key proves the tokens are yours.

  • 📝

    Never share your seed phrase. Nobody can reset it.

  • 🎣

    Watch for scams. Real support never asks for it.

  • 🧺

    ETFs need no wallet. Your broker holds them.

Not advice.
